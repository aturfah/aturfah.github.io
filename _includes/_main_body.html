About

I am a Ph.D candidate in the Biostatistics Department at the University of Michigan co-advized by Dr. William Wen and Dr. Lili Zhao. My dissertation research focuses on new methods for cluster analysis to help improve analyses of biomedical data, with applications in COVID-19 vaccine safety studies and single-cell RNA expression data.

I completed my undergraduate studies in Data Science Engineering from the University of Michigan College of Engineering in 2018, and completed my M.A. in Statistics from Columbia University in 2021.
